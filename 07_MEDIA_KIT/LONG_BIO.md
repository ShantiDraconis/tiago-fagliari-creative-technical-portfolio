# Long Bio

Tiago Fagliari is an architecture-trained Creative Technical Director and Senior Lighting Technician working across cruise entertainment, theatre, festivals, hospitality, and immersive live experiences. His practice connects creative vision with technical delivery through lighting systems, show control, rehearsal discipline, and multidisciplinary operations.

Under the creative identity Shanti Draconis, he develops concept architecture, lighting narrative, and spatial storytelling frameworks for ceremonies, destination experiences, and large-scale productions.
