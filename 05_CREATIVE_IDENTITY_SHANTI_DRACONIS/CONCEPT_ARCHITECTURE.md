# Concept Architecture

Concept architecture connects spatial design, audience flow, atmosphere, production logic, and the practical systems required to deliver a live experience.

For Shanti Draconis, concept architecture means translating an idea into a spatial and technical structure that can be rehearsed, operated, maintained, and remembered.

## Focus

- Spatial identity
- Audience journey
- Hospitality and destination atmosphere
- Lighting and media integration
- Operational feasibility
- Creative-technical communication
