# Colors

## Direction

The visual system should feel professional, cinematic, technical, and recruiter-facing without becoming decorative or opaque.

## Suggested Palette

- Deep charcoal for primary backgrounds and text emphasis.
- Warm off-white for readable documents.
- Controlled amber or gold accents for lighting identity.
- Neutral gray for technical metadata and dividers.
- Optional deep green or muted red accents for stage and signal references.

## Use

Prioritize clarity, legibility, and consistent hierarchy over heavy visual effects.
