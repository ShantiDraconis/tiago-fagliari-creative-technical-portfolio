# Transparent Overlay Guide

## Purpose

Transparent overlays may be used for portfolio stills, showreel frames, case study thumbnails, and recruiter-facing visual summaries.

## Content

- Name and role.
- Production or case study label.
- System focus such as lighting, show control, media, stage, or hospitality flow.
- Link or QR code only when final public links are available.

## Safety

Avoid exposing proprietary show files, private documents, console details that are confidential, internal company materials, or personal information.
