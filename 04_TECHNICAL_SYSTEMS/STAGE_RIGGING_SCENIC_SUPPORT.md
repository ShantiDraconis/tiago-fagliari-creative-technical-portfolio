# Stage / Rigging / Scenic Support

## Scope

Stage, scenic, backstage, and rigging-support experience across theatre, cruise entertainment, festivals, and live events.

## Capabilities

- Stage preparation and backstage coordination.
- Scenic support, props, special effects, and transitions.
- Fly/counterweight system awareness.
- Rehearsal and reset support.
- Safe handling, crew communication, and show continuity.
